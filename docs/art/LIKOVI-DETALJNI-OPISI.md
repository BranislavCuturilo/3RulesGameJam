# 🏛️ BOGOVI (toweri)

## 1. Perun — grom · Ranged + AOE_Stun
**Lore:** Vrhovni bog groma, rata i pravde. Zreo ratnik **bakarno-crvene/zlatne brade**, srebrne kose. Nosi **sekiru** i luk koji izbacuje munje. Simboli: **thundermark** (gromoviti pečat), **orao**, **hrast**, bik. Vozi kočiju koju vuku jarci.
**Vizuelni opis:** Krupan bradati bog u gravisanom zlatno-čeličnom gotičkom oklopu, bakarna brada, na ramenima motiv orla, na grudima urezan thundermark (šestokraki točak munje). U ruci bojna sekira oko koje pucketaju plavo-bele munje. Olujno-sivi plašt, varnice.
**PixelLab:**
```
Perun the Slavic thunder god, mature broad warrior deity, copper-red braided beard, silver hair,
ornate gilded gothic plate armor, eagle-motif pauldrons, engraved thundermark sigil on chest,
wielding a great battle axe crackling with blue-white lightning, storm-grey cloak,
dark gothic pixel art, somber palette with gold, steel and electric blue accents
```

## 2. Svarog — vatra/kovač · Melee (smash) + AOE_Impact + DOT ✅ napravljen
**Lore:** Bog vatre, neba i **kovačkog zanata**, otac bogova. Prikazivan i kao starac duge sede kose i kao snažan ratnik-zaštitnik. **Uvek nosi čekić** (alat i oružje). Simbol: **Kolovrat** (sunčev točak).
**Vizuelni opis:** Snažan bradati bog-kovač, gravirani zlatni gotički oklop sa kovačkom keceljom, glowing kovački čekić, na oklopu urezan Kolovrat, žar i iskre, tamno-crveni plašt.
**PixelLab:**
```
Svarog the Slavic god of fire and the celestial forge, strong bearded blacksmith deity,
ornate gilded gothic plate armor with a forge apron, engraved kolovrat sun-wheel emblem,
holding a glowing forge war hammer, drifting embers, dark crimson cloak,
dark gothic pixel art, somber palette with gold and deep crimson accents
```

## 3. Dažbog — sunce · Ranged (snajper, DPS)
**Lore:** Sin Svaroga, **bog sunca**, toplote, blagostanja. Prikazivan u **zlatnom oklopu** sa **blistavim štitom** koji odražava sunčeve zrake.
**Vizuelni opis:** Plemenit bog u sjajnom zlatnom gotičkom oklopu, iza glave **solarni disk/oreol**, u ruci **koplje/luk od svetlosti** ili blistavi štit. Topli zlatni zraci, malo tame okolo radi kontrasta (Blasphemous).
**PixelLab:**
```
Dazhbog the Slavic sun god, radiant noble deity in shining golden gothic armor,
a blazing solar disk halo behind his head, holding a spear of golden light and a radiant sun shield,
warm golden glow against a dark background, dark gothic pixel art,
somber palette with bright gold and amber accents
```

## 4. Morana — zima/smrt · Ranged + Slow
**Lore:** Boginja **zime, smrti i pošasti**. **Dvojna priroda**: bleda devojka duge tamne kose sa vučjim očnjacima — ili usahla starica. Nosi **srp/kosu**. Suprotnost Vesni (proleće).
**Vizuelni opis:** Mršava bleda veštica-boginja, crna kosa, ledena kruna, **mrazom prekrivena crna haljina**, u ruci **zaleđeni srp/kosa**, dah magle, blago naznake truleži. Hladna plavo-bela paleta sa srebrom.
**PixelLab:**
```
Morana the Slavic goddess of winter, death and pestilence, gaunt pale sorceress, long black hair,
icy jagged crown, frost-crusted tattered black gown, holding a frozen scythe, cold misty breath,
subtle signs of decay, dark gothic pixel art, somber palette with pale ice blue, silver and faint gold
```

## 5. Veles — podzemlje/otrov · Ranged + DOT_AOE (NE summoner)
**Lore:** Htonski bog **podzemlja, magije, stoke i bogatstva**, protivnik Peruna. Rogati (jelenski/bikovski rogovi), u **medveđem krznu**, duga brada, delom zmijolik, vezan za vlagu i blato. (Mitološki šejpšifter, ali u igri = bacač otrova, ne priziva.)
**Vizuelni opis:** Mračni rogati šaman-bog, **jelenski rogovi**, ogrtač od medveđe kože, gnarled drveni štap, oko nogu se savija zmija, **zelenkasti sjaj podzemlja**, vlažne/blatnjave teksture, zlatni prstenovi (bogatstvo).
**PixelLab:**
```
Veles the Slavic god of the underworld, magic and cattle, dark horned shaman deity, elk antlers,
long beard, heavy bear-hide cloak, holding a gnarled wooden staff, a serpent coiling at his feet,
eerie green underworld glow, damp earthy textures, gold rings, dark gothic pixel art,
somber palette with murky green, brown and gold accents
```

## 6. Stribog — vetar · Ranged + AOE_Front (konus)
**Lore:** Bog **vetra** koji razdeljuje bogatstvo; svi vetrovi su mu unuci. Starac u **dugim belim odorama**, nosi **veliki rog** kojim doziva vetrove, i **luk sa strelama** (brze kao vetar). Među oblacima.
**Vizuelni opis:** Vitak sedi starac, **vetrom razvejane pohabane odore**, u ruci veliki zavijeni rog, oko njega kovitlac vazduha i listova, luk preko ramena. Sivo-tirkizna paleta.
**PixelLab:**
```
Stribog the Slavic god of wind, lean elderly deity with long white beard, flowing wind-torn robes,
holding a great curved horn to summon the winds, a bow on his back, swirling gusts of air around him,
dark gothic pixel art, somber palette with pale teal, grey and faint gold accents
```

## 7. Jarilo — proleće · Melee (brz slash) + DOT
**Lore:** Mladi bog **proleća, vegetacije, plodnosti** (i ratoborne mladosti). Lep mladić, često **bos**, u **belom ogrtaču**, na belom konju, **ovenčan klasjem i cvećem**, nosi klasje/srp.
**Vizuelni opis:** Mladi ratnik-bog, kruna od **pšenice i cveća**, belo-zeleni ogrtač, u ruci **zakrivljeni srp** (oružje), oko njega vinova loza i zlatno klasje, energična poza. Živo zelena + zlato.
**PixelLab:**
```
Jarilo the Slavic god of spring and fertility, youthful handsome warrior, crown of wheat and flowers,
white and green cloak, barefoot, holding a curved sickle blade, green vines and golden grain around him,
dark gothic pixel art, somber palette with vivid green and gold accents
```

## 8. Mokoš — zemlja/sudbina · Ranged + Stun/AOE_Slow (NE heal)
**Lore:** Boginja **zemlje, plodnosti, vode i sudbine**; **prede nit sudbine**. Prikazivana **velike glave** (mudrost) i **dugih ispruženih ruku**; simboli **vreteno, nit, razboj**, ponekad rog izobilja. Vezana za izvore/vlagu. (U igri = vezivanje/korenje, ne leči.)
**Vizuelni opis:** Matronska figura, **velika glava**, vezene zemljane odore, u rukama **vreteno i nit** (zlatna nit sudbine), oko nogu korenje i loza, vlažne zemljane teksture.
**PixelLab:**
```
Mokosh the Slavic earth and fate goddess, matronly figure with a large head, long outstretched arms,
embroidered earthen robes, holding a spindle and golden thread of fate, roots and vines at her feet,
dark gothic pixel art, somber palette with earthy brown, deep green and gold accents
```

## 9. Svetovid — rat/proročanstvo · Melee (zamah mačem) + AOE_Front
**Lore:** Četvoroglavi bog (**4 glave gledaju na 4 strane sveta** — "onaj koji vidi svet"). U ruci **rog** (proricanje), luk, o boku **srebrni mač**. Sveti **beli konj**.
**Vizuelni opis:** Strog **četvoroglavi** ratnik (dva lica napred, dva pozadi), belo-zlatni gotički oklop, **srebrni mač** u zamahu, rog za pojasom, blistava bela svetlost. (Za top-down: 4 lica vidljiva po obodu glave.)
**PixelLab:**
```
Svetovid the Slavic four-headed war god, stern warrior with four faces looking in all directions,
white and gold ornate gothic armor, swinging a gleaming silver sword, a drinking horn at his belt,
radiant white light, dark gothic pixel art, somber palette with white, silver and gold accents
```

## 10. Triglav — tri sveta · Ranged + AOE_Impact (arkana)
**Lore:** **Troglavi** bog (nebo, zemlja, podzemlje — vlast nad tri sveta). Oči mu (po nekim izvorima) **prekrivene zlatnim velom** da ne vidi ljudske grehe. Vezan za proročanstvo (sveti konj).
**Vizuelni opis:** Dostojanstven **troglavi** bog u raskošnim arkanim odorama, **oči prekrivene zlatnim velom/povezom**, u ruci zlatni štap, ljubičasta arkana aura, tri krune.
**PixelLab:**
```
Triglav the Slavic three-headed god, dignified three-faced deity, eyes covered with golden veils,
ornate arcane robes, three crowns, holding a golden staff, glowing purple arcane aura,
dark gothic pixel art, somber palette with deep purple and gold accents
```

# 👹 ČUDOVIŠTA (enemies)

## 11. Drekavac — brz/swarm
**Lore:** "Vrištalac" — nastao iz duša **nekrštene dece/grešnika**. Telo **izduženo i tanko kao vreteno**, **nesrazmerno velika glava**, opisivan i kao pseće/ptičije biće sa **zadnjim nogama kao u kengura**. Vidljiv noću; jeziv vrisak.
**Vizuelni opis:** Mršav, košćat noćni demon, **prevelika glava razjapljenih usta** (vrisak), tanki dugi udovi, kandže, pseće-ptičiji hibrid, **užarene oči**, senovito sivkasto telo, slab bolešljiv sjaj.
**PixelLab:**
```
Drekavac a screaming Slavic night demon, emaciated body thin as a spindle, disproportionately large
screaming head with gaping mouth, long thin clawed limbs, dog-bird hybrid, glowing eyes, feral,
dark gothic pixel art, somber palette with sickly grey, shadow and faint pale glow
```

## 12. Psoglav — tank
**Lore:** Himera iz srpskog folklora: **pseća glava sa jednim okom i gvozdenim zubima**, **ljudski torzo**, **konjske zadnje noge**. Živi u mračnim pećinama punim dragulja; ljudožder, kopa grobove.
**Vizuelni opis:** Glomazni **pseće-glavi** ljudožder, **jedno cikloposko oko**, **gvozdeni zubi**, mišićav ljudski torzo, **konjske zadnje noge**, oklop od kostiju/gvožđa, prljavština groba, gvozdeno-bronzane boje.
**PixelLab:**
```
Psoglav a Serbian dog-headed ogre demon, single large cyclops eye, iron teeth, snarling canine head,
hulking muscular human torso, equine hind legs, crude bone and iron armor, grave dirt,
dark gothic pixel art, somber palette with iron grey, dull bronze and blood accents
```

## 13. Aždaja — BOSS (zmaj) · `body_type: quadruped`
**Lore:** **Demonski zmaj** (mračna verzija "zmaja"). **Neparan broj glava** (nikad paran — npr. 3), **bljuje vatru**, riče da sve podrhtava. Živi u planinskim pećinama, sleće na sela.
**Vizuelni opis:** Ogroman **troglavi** demonski zmaj, tri zmijske glave, **krljušt boje ugljenisane crveno-crne**, rogovi i kandže, opaljena krila, **vatreni dah**, planinska zver.
**PixelLab:** (`body_type: quadruped`, template `lion`/`bear`, size 128)
```

deep red and black scales, horns and claws, ragged burnt wings, breathing fire, menacing mountain beast,
dark gothic pixel art, somber palette with deep red, charred black and gold accents
```
